About This Simulator

Volatility Trading Academy is a self-contained, client-side paper-trading education tool. No real money, no real market data, no backend.

Risk & Educational Disclaimer

This site is a paper-trading education tool only. Every ticker, price, and company name is entirely fictional and generated by a simulated random-walk price engine running in your browser — none of it reflects real securities, real market data, or real market conditions.

Nothing on this site is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security. The "cash" and "P&L" you see are simulated numbers stored only in your browser's local storage — no real money is ever involved, and nothing here should be used to make real trading or investment decisions.

Volatile strategies like short selling, leveraged ETFs, and penny stocks carry substantial real-world risk of loss, including in some cases losses beyond your initial investment. This simulator is designed to teach the mechanics and risk concepts of those strategies in a safe environment — always do independent research and consider consulting a licensed financial professional before trading with real capital.

How the Price Engine Works

Every ticker's price is generated by a geometric-Brownian-motion style random walk: each price tick applies a random return drawn from a normal distribution, shaped by that ticker's own annualized volatility and drift parameters. Blue-chip sims use low volatility, momentum and penny-stock sims use high volatility, and a couple of tickers include rare "jump" events to simulate earnings surprises or pump-and-dump spikes. A seeded generator builds a believable multi-day price history the first time you load the site, and a live tick engine (about every 1.5 seconds) keeps prices moving while you browse. Nothing is fetched from the internet — it's all computed locally in your browser.

How the Trading & Portfolio Engine Works

New accounts start with $100,000 in simulated cash. Buying deducts cash and opens or adds to a long position with a weighted-average cost basis; selling closes a long position, credits cash, and realizes profit or loss. Shorting credits proceeds to your cash balance but requires your account equity to cover at least 150% of your total short exposure — matching typical real-world Reg T short-margin rules — before the order is accepted. Covering a short debits cash and realizes the profit or loss on that position. Market orders fill instantly at the live simulated price; limit orders queue and are automatically checked against the price on every tick, filling the moment the market price crosses your limit. Every order — filled, open, cancelled, or rejected — is recorded in your order history, and your full account state (cash, positions, orders, equity history) is saved to your browser's local storage so it survives page reloads and navigation.

Reset Account
